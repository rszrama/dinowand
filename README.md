# Dino Wand

This is a simple FPS built with Grok Bot. Dinosaurs spawn and will alert their friends and charge at you when they see you. Kill enough and pterodactyls will appear ... and eventually the T-Rex. Collect coins from chests and kills then buy new wands, defensive turrets, and even extra hearts to absorb more damage before dying.

Good luck!

Play it here: https://rszrama.github.io/dinowand/
